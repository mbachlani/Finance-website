About Malik Bachlani

I believe everyone deserves financial confidence — regardless of where they start. When I joined World Financial Group, my goal was simple: to empower people with knowledge and opportunities that change lives.

Over the years, I’ve helped families across Canada plan for education, retirement, and long-term security. I’ve also mentored individuals who wanted more from their careers — freedom, flexibility, and fulfillment — to become successful entrepreneurs in financial services.

💡 My mission: To help 1,000 families and 100 entrepreneurs achieve financial independence and legacy.
